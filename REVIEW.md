# The F130 decision

This is the only thing that has to come back as a yes or a no.

**The claim in circulation:** penalties go live on 13 November 2026.

**Why people say it:** clause (b) of G.S.R. 843(E) commences section 27(1)(d) on that
date, and 27(1)(d) contains the words "to inquire into such breach and impose penalty".

**Our reading:** clause (c) of the same notification holds sections 28 to 34 back to
13 May 2027. Section 33 with the Schedule is the penalty power. Section 28 is what lets
the Board act at all. 27(1)(d) is limited to a Consent Manager breaching its own
registration conditions, and every route to an ordinary Data Fiduciary sits in
27(1)(a), (b), (c) or (e), all of which commence in May 2027. So on 13 November 2026 the
Board holds one function, pointing at a penalty provision not in force, with no procedure
to exercise it, against a class of entity that cannot exist until that same day.

**Conclusion, as the deck currently states it:** no penalty can be imposed on anyone on
13 November 2026, and no Data Fiduciary is within reach of the Board's inquiry power on
that date.

**The honest caveat:** this is a construction of the notified text, not a fact in the way
the rest of the fact base uses that word. `F131` sets out the strongest contrary argument
rather than pretending it does not exist.

Read `F123` to `F131` in `facts/dpdp-facts.md` against
`facts/source-gsr-843E-act-commencement.txt`, then answer:

> **Will you put your name on this reading and take the question if one comes?**

**If yes,** the deck asserts it as it does now, and your name goes on slide 7.

**If no,** the block softens and does not break. It shows what the notification lists,
then what commentators infer from it, then the dependency chain as an open question, and
lets the room draw its own conclusion. Weaker, and still more accurate than anything else
that room will have heard on the subject.

Two slides need different wording depending on the answer, which is why this has to be
settled before the deck is finalised rather than after.
