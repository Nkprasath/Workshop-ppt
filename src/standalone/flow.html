  1. 1. Explain the slides for that block.

Block 1 · The timeline · 0:09 to 0:26

0:09 Slide 4 One Act, three commencement dates Olivia 4 min

Say: The Act comes into force in three stages. Left: in force since 13 November 2025. Middle: 13 November 2026, two provisions on Consent Managers. Right: 13 May 2027, the obligations most businesses must comply with.

Note: Point out the asterisk: if anyone intends to register as a Consent Manager, the November date applies to them. From here on, say DPBI for the Data Protection Board of India.

0:13 Slide 5 What comes into force on 13 November 2026 Olivia 3 min

Say: Two provisions: Consent Manager duties, and the DPBI's power to register them. Inquiries and penalties come into force on 13 May 2027.

0:16 Slide 6 Who 13 November 2026 applies to Olivia 3 min

Say: Organisations that intend to register as Consent Managers. Registration is voluntary, and needs an appointed DPBI and published conditions, neither of which exists yet.

0:19 Slide 7 What enforcement requires Olivia 4 min

Say: Three things must be in place: an appointed DPBI, a power to inquire and a power to impose penalties. Both powers come into force on 13 May 2027, so that is the date to prepare for.

Expect: Someone may say they read that penalties start in November. Answer from the schedule on slide 4.
