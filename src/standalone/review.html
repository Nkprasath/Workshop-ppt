Background: why the deck says penalties start in May 2027

The claim in circulation. Penalties go live on 13 November 2026, the twelve month mark.

Why people say it. Clause (b) of G.S.R. 843(E) commences section 27(1)(d) on that date, and 27(1)(d) reads:

"on receipt of an intimation of breach of any condition of registration of a Consent Manager, to inquire into such breach and impose penalty as provided in this Act"

People see "impose penalty" commencing on that date and stop reading.

What the same notification also says. Clause (c) of that instrument holds back to 13 May 2027:

"sections 3 to 5, sub-sections (1) to (8) and (10) of section 6, sections 7 to 10, sections 11 to 17, section 27 except clause (d) of sub-section (1) of the said section, sections 28 to 34, 36, 37 and sub-section (2) of section 44"

Four things follow:

  1. Section 33 is the penalty power, and the Schedule setting the amounts is headed "[See section 33(1)]". Both are in the eighteen month tranche, so 27(1)(d)'s phrase "as provided in this Act" points at a provision that is not yet in force.
  2. Section 28 is what lets the Board act at all. Also May 2027. The Board would hold a function with no procedure to exercise it.
  3. 27(1)(d) concerns only a Consent Manager breaching its own registration conditions. Every route by which the Board could reach an ordinary Data Fiduciary sits in 27(1)(a), (b), (c) or (e), and all four commence in May 2027.
  4. There is nobody to inquire into on day one in any case, because Consent Manager registration under s.6(9) with Rule 4 only opens on that same date.

The conclusion the deck states. No penalty can be imposed on anyone on 13 November 2026, and no Data Fiduciary is within reach of the Board's inquiry power on that date. The penalty machinery arrives with everything else on 13 May 2027.

The honest caveat

This is a construction of the notified text, not a fact in the way the rest of our fact base uses that word. The strongest contrary argument is that 27(1)(d) is self-executing, so the Board could inquire into a Consent Manager registration breach from November even without section 28. Even on that reading no monetary penalty follows, because the amount is set only by section 33 with the Schedule and neither is in force. Worth knowing the shape of it, in case it comes up from the floor.
